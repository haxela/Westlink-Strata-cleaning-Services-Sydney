A Practical Approach

What Is Strata Cleaning?

Strata cleaning focuses on the shared areas of apartment buildings, townhouses and other managed properties. These spaces may include entrances, corridors, staircases, lifts, shared bathrooms, bin rooms and outdoor paths.

A clear cleaning plan helps keep these areas tidy and usable. The right tasks and visit frequency depend on property size, resident numbers, foot traffic, surface types and the priorities set by the strata manager or committee.

Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

These answers cover common points to discuss when arranging shared-property cleaning.

What does strata cleaning usually include?

It can include agreed cleaning tasks for shared entrances, lobbies, corridors, stairwells, lifts, floors, glass, bin rooms and outdoor common areas. The final scope depends on the property and service agreement.

How often should common areas be cleaned?

There is no single schedule for every building. Foot traffic, resident numbers, facilities and the strata committee's priorities all affect the right frequency.

What affects the cost of strata cleaning?

Pricing may depend on property size, the number and type of common areas, required tasks, visit frequency, surface types and any specialist cleaning requested. Ask for a quote based on your building's needs.

Can carpet and outdoor surface cleaning be included?

These tasks can be discussed as part of the scope. The suitable method depends on the carpet or surface material, its condition and access to the area.
